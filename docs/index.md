## Introduction

This datasheet documents the design and development of the pressure pad subsystem for the team project. The purpose of the pressure pad subsystem is to detect when pressure is applied to the pad and provide a signal that can be processed by the microcontroller.

### Project Summary

The team project consists of the development of an interactive cat toy. My portion of the project is the pressure pad subsystem, which is responsible for detecting when the cat applies pressure to the pad.

More information about the complete team project can be found on the [Team 203 Project Website](https://egr304-2026-f-203.github.io/EGR304_Team203.github.io/).

### My Contribution

My contribution to the team project is the design and development of the pressure pad  subsystem. This subsystem is incharge of detecting when there is pressure applied on the sensor, it sends a signal to the microcontroler to power on the whole system.
