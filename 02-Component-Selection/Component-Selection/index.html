    Major Component Selection

    Pressure Sensor

    Table 1: Pressure Sensor Selection

    Solution Pros Cons

    Option 1
    FSR01CE
    Ohmite Force Sensing Resistor
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Large sensing area is well suited for the pressure pad.
    Thin construction makes it easy to install under the pressure surface.
     Resistance output can be converted to voltage using a simple voltage divider.
    Requires relatively little supporting circuitry.
     Simple analog output is easy to interface with the signal-conditioning circuit.    		 * Resistance-to-force relationship is nonlinear.
    Less accurate than precision force sensors such as load cells.
     Output can vary depending on how force is distributed across the sensing area.
    * Calibration is required for accurate force measurements.

    Option 2
    FSR02CE
    Ohmite Force Sensing Resistor
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Compact size requires little installation space.
    Thin construction simplifies mechanical integration.
     Resistance output allows simple analog signal conditioning.
    * Requires few supporting electrical components.    		 * Smaller sensing area is less suitable for the pressure pad.
    Force must be concentrated over a smaller area.
     Resistance-to-force relationship is nonlinear.
    * Requires calibration for accurate force measurements.

    Option 3
    FSR03CE
    Ohmite Force Sensing Resistor
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Larger sensing area than the FSR02CE.
    Thin construction allows installation underneath the pressure surface.
     Resistance output is easy to interface with analog circuitry.
    * Requires relatively few supporting components.    		 * Sensing area is smaller than the FSR01CE.
    Nonlinear response makes precise force measurements difficult.
     Requires calibration for quantitative measurements.
    * Output can vary with force distribution.

    Choice: Option 1: FSR01CE

    Rationale: The FSR01CE was selected because its larger sensing area is better suited for the pressure-pad subsystem. Its resistance-based output can be converted into a voltage using a simple voltage divider and conditioned before being measured by the microcontroller. The subsystem primarily needs to detect changes in applied pressure rather than make precision force measurements, making the FSR01CE a suitable combination of sensing area, simple interfacing, and mechanical integration.

    Operational Amplifier

    Table 2: Operational Amplifier Selection

    Solution Pros Cons

    Option 1
    MCP6004-I/P
    Microchip Technology
    Quad Operational Amplifier
    14-PDIP Through-Hole
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Operates from 1.8 V to 6 V and is compatible with the 5 V rail.
    Rail-to-rail output provides a large usable output-voltage range.
     Four operational amplifiers are included in one IC.
    Low supply current reduces system power consumption.
     Through-hole PDIP package is easy to solder.
    * Extra amplifier channels allow future expansion.    		 * 1 MHz gain-bandwidth product is lower than higher-performance alternatives.
    Four amplifier channels may be more than currently required.
     14-pin package occupies more PCB space than an 8-pin dual amplifier.
    * Cannot operate directly from supply voltages greater than 6 V.

    Option 2
    MCP6002-I/P
    Microchip Technology
    Dual Operational Amplifier
    8-PDIP Through-Hole
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Operates from 1.8 V to 6 V and is compatible with the 5 V rail.
    Rail-to-rail output is suitable for interfacing with the microcontroller ADC.
     Smaller 8-pin package requires less PCB space.
    Low supply current reduces system power consumption.
     Through-hole package simplifies soldering and assembly.    		 * Contains only two amplifier channels compared with four in the MCP6004.
    Future expansion could require an additional IC.
     1 MHz bandwidth is lower than higher-performance alternatives.
    * Provides fewer amplifier channels while offering similar electrical performance to the MCP6004.

    Option 3
    TLV2464IN
    Texas Instruments
    Quad Operational Amplifier
    14-PDIP Through-Hole
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Contains four operational amplifiers in one package.
    Compatible with the 5 V power rail.
     Rail-to-rail output provides a large usable output range.
    6.4 MHz bandwidth provides greater performance than the MCP6004.
     Through-hole package simplifies manual PCB assembly.    		 * More expensive than the MCP6004 and MCP6002.
    Higher supply current increases system power consumption.
     Additional bandwidth is unnecessary for the slowly changing pressure-pad signal.
    * 14-pin package provides no PCB-space advantage over the MCP6004.

    Choice: Option 1: MCP6004-I/P

    Rationale: The MCP6004-I/P was selected because it provides four operational amplifiers, rail-to-rail output, low power consumption, and direct compatibility with the 5 V power rail. The MCP6002-I/P requires less PCB space but only provides two amplifier channels. The TLV2464IN provides greater bandwidth, but this additional performance is unnecessary for the relatively slow pressure-pad signal. The MCP6004-I/P therefore provides the required performance while also providing additional amplifier channels for future signal-conditioning requirements.

    5 V Voltage Regulator

    Table 3: Voltage Regulator Selection

    Solution Pros Cons

    Option 1
    MC7805CTG
    onsemi
    5 V Linear Voltage Regulator
    TO-220 Through-Hole
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Provides the regulated 5 V output required by the subsystem.
    Through-hole TO-220 package simplifies manual soldering.
     High output-current capability provides substantial current margin.
    Common regulator architecture simplifies circuit implementation.
     Internal protection features improve reliability.    		 * Excess input voltage is dissipated as heat.
    Requires an input voltage sufficiently above 5 V to maintain regulation.
     Efficiency decreases as the difference between input and output voltage increases.
    * Thermal management may be required at high load currents.

    Option 2
    UA7805CKCS
    Texas Instruments
    5 V Linear Voltage Regulator
    TO-220 Through-Hole
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Fixed 5 V output directly matches the required power rail.
    High output-current capability provides significant design margin.
     Internal thermal protection improves reliability.
    Internal current limiting provides protection during fault conditions.
     Through-hole TO-220 package simplifies assembly.    		 * Linear regulation dissipates excess input voltage as heat.
    Input voltage must remain sufficiently above 5 V.
     Current capability is much greater than required by this subsystem.
    * Less efficient than a switching regulator when operating from a higher input voltage.

    Option 3
    LM2931T-5.0/NOPB
    Texas Instruments
    5 V Low-Dropout Linear Regulator
    TO-220 Through-Hole
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Fixed 5 V output matches the required system voltage.
    Low-dropout design can operate with a lower input voltage than conventional 7805 regulators.
     Through-hole TO-220 package simplifies manual soldering.
    * Lower dropout can reduce wasted voltage when the input supply is close to 5 V.    		 * Lower maximum output current than the 7805 alternatives.
    Provides less current margin for future expansion.
     Still dissipates excess input voltage as heat.
    * Output-current capability must be verified using the final power budget.

    Choice: Option 1: MC7805CTG

    Rationale: The MC7805CTG was selected because it provides the required regulated 5 V output and sufficient current capacity for the pressure-pad subsystem while maintaining additional current margin. Its through-hole TO-220 package is appropriate for manual PCB assembly. The LM2931 provides lower dropout but has less output-current capability, while the UA7805CKCS provides more current capability than the subsystem requires. The MC7805CTG therefore provides a simple and robust solution when paired with an appropriate external DC supply.

    5.5 mm OD / 2.0 mm ID
    Through-Hole DC Barrel Jack
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Through-hole mounting provides good mechanical strength.
    Right-angle orientation is convenient for PCB-edge mounting.
     Electrical ratings exceed the expected subsystem requirements.
    * Integrated switching contact provides additional power-routing options.    		 * 2.0 mm center pin is less compatible with common 2.1 mm plugs.
    Requires a specifically matched external power adapter.
     Occupies valuable PCB-edge space.
    * Does not protect the circuit from an incorrect supply voltage or polarity.

    Option 3
    PJ-002A
    Same Sky
    5.5 mm OD / 2.0 mm ID
    Through-Hole DC Barrel Jack
    DigiKey Product Page    		 * Through-hole construction provides good mechanical strength.
    Electrical ratings provide substantial margin over the expected subsystem load.
     Right-angle configuration allows convenient PCB-edge access.
    * Simple construction makes schematic integration straightforward.    		 * 2.0 mm center pin requires a matching external plug.
    Less compatible with common 2.1 mm adapters than the PJ-202A.
     Physical connector occupies significant PCB-edge space.
    * Does not provide protection against incorrect external supply voltage or polarity.

    Choice: Option 1: PJ-202A

    Rationale: The PJ-202A was selected because its through-hole construction provides a strong mechanical connection to the PCB and its right-angle orientation allows convenient access from the edge of the board. Its 5.5 mm outer diameter and 2.1 mm center-pin configuration provides a practical connection for an external DC power adapter. Its electrical ratings also provide substantial margin over the requirements of the pressure-pad subsystem. Compared with the other two options, the PJ-202A provides the preferred 2.1 mm center-pin connection for the planned external power supply.
